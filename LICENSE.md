# Independent Research and Verification

A significant portion of the information presented in this guide has been independently compiled, tested, compared, and verified by the repository author.

This includes, among other things:

- testing text-formatting codes directly within the game;
- testing color and icon combinations;
- verifying the behavior of individual tags;
- identifying functional and non-functional tags;
- identifying duplicate tags and references;
- documenting observed behavior;
- organizing the collected information into structured reference tables;
